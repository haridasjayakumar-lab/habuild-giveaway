# HABUILD 21-DAY JUNE CHALLENGE
## Referral Growth Proposal

### Objective
Drive organic referrals through the Facebook community during a 21-day June challenge by turning existing members into health ambassadors.

**Core philosophy:** Referrals should feel like an act of care, not a sales pitch.

## Campaign Pillars

### PILLAR 1: Healing Link — "Sahi Yoga, Sahi Insaan Tak"
#### What is it?

A simple tool (app / web page / WhatsApp flow) that lets members send personalised yoga invites based on the specific health problem their friend or family member is facing. One tap, one share — no thinking required.

#### How it works:

Members open the tool and select from a list of common ailments:

- Knee Pain
- Back Pain
- Body Pain / Stiffness
- Stress / Anxiety
- Weight Management
- Diabetes / BP Management
- Neck & Shoulder Pain
- Sleep Issues

They tap on the relevant ailment and a pre-written, personalised message is generated. For example:

> "Hey, I know you've been dealing with back pain. I've been doing yoga with Habuild and it has genuinely helped me. They have specific sessions for back pain relief. Try it for free — here's the link: [link]. Trust me, you'll feel the difference in a week."

One tap to copy. One tap to send via WhatsApp, SMS, or Facebook Messenger.

#### How it drives referrals:

- Removes friction completely — members don't have to think about what to say
- Personalisation increases conversion — a message about "your knee pain" hits harder than a generic "try yoga"
- Specificity builds trust — the receiver sees this isn't spam, it's someone who thought about their specific problem
- Scales effortlessly — one tool, unlimited shares

### PILLAR 2: Health Ka Tohfa — "Gift Health This June"
#### What is it?

A campaign-wide framing that positions every invite as a gift. June becomes the month where members "gift health" to someone they love.

#### How it works:

Create a branded campaign identity: "Health Ka Tohfa"

Design shareable graphics (WhatsApp-friendly, vertical format) that members can forward:
- "Iss June, kisi apne ko health ka tohfa do."
- "Meri taraf se ek gift — 21 din ka free yoga. Accept karo."

Run daily prompts in the Facebook group:
- Day 1: "Kisne aaj health ka tohfa bheja? Comment mein batao!"
- Day 7: "7 din ho gaye — kya aapne abhi tak kisi ko tohfa diya?"
- Day 14: "Sirf 7 din bache hain. Abhi bhi waqt hai."

Create a "Tohfa Counter" — track and display total invites sent by the community as a collective milestone.

#### How it drives referrals:

- "Gift" framing triggers generosity, not obligation
- Emotional language ("tohfa", "apne") resonates deeply with Hindi-speaking audience
- Daily prompts create sustained momentum — not a one-time ask but a 21-day drumbeat
- Public tracking creates accountability — no one wants to be the person who didn't participate

### PILLAR 3: Before/After Member Posters — "Mera Badlaav"
#### What is it?

Members create and share their own transformation posters — visual proof of what Habuild has done for them.

#### How it works:

Provide a simple poster template (Canva link or in-app tool) where members fill in:
- Their photo (optional) or an avatar
- "Before Habuild" vs "After Habuild" — not just physical, but energy, mood, pain levels, sleep quality
- A one-line quote, e.g.: "6 months ago I couldn't touch my toes. Today I do surya namaskar every morning."
- The Habuild joining link at the bottom

Encourage members to post in the Facebook group AND on their personal profiles and stories

Feature the best posters daily with a "Transformation of the Day" spotlight

Run a "Best Transformation Poster" contest — community votes for the most inspiring story

#### How it drives referrals:

- Visual proof is the most powerful marketing — a member's real story beats any ad
- Personal social media sharing extends reach beyond the Facebook group into each member's own network
- User-generated content is trusted 3x more than brand content
- Contest element gamifies participation and creates urgency to post

### PILLAR 4: Yoga Buddy — "Saath Mein Shuru, Saath Mein Badhein"
#### What is it?

When someone new joins through a friend's link, both are paired together as "Yoga Buddies" for the full 21 days. Both are accountable to each other, both grow together.

#### How it works:

When someone new signs up through a friend's link, both are tagged as a Yoga Buddy pair

Both receive a daily check-in prompt: "Aaj aapne aur aapke buddy ne yoga kiya?"

Buddy pairs who complete all 21 days together get a "21-Day Buddy Badge" and a shoutout

Create a "Buddy Wall" in the Facebook group where pairs post together, tag each other, and cheer each other on

Weekly spotlight: "Buddy Pair of the Week" — the most active and consistent pair gets featured

#### How it drives referrals:

- Members become invested in their friend's journey — they don't just invite and forget
- New members are 3x more likely to stick when they have a friend doing it alongside them
- Solves two problems at once — brings in new members AND keeps them engaged
- Creates emotional stories — buddy pairs finishing 21 days together is powerful content for future campaigns
- Triggers a second wave — members who had a great buddy experience will want to invite their own friends next

## Additional Ideas to Amplify Referrals

5. **"Referral Streak" Challenge** Members who invite someone on 3 consecutive days unlock a special badge or a shoutout from the instructor during the live session. Streaks create habit loops — once someone invites one person, the mechanic nudges them to keep going.

6. **Instructor-Led Invite Moments** During live yoga sessions, the instructor pauses for 30 seconds and says: "Abhi session ke baad, ek kaam karo — ek insaan ko link bhejo jisko iska zaroorat hai." A direct ask from a trusted voice during a moment of high engagement is incredibly effective.

7. **"My Yoga Story" 60-Second Video Challenge** Ask members to record a 60-second video about what yoga changed for them and share it on social media with the joining link. Video content gets 2–3x more reach than text or image posts. Feature the best videos in the group.

8. **Milestone Celebrations** At community milestones (50, 100, 200 new members), unlock something special — a bonus yoga session, a live Q&A with a guest instructor, or a fun community challenge. This turns inviting friends into a collective mission, not an individual task.

9. **"Sabse Bada Health Ambassador" Title** At the end of 21 days, crown the most active member as the community's Health Ambassador. Give them recognition in the group, a certificate, and a feature on Habuild's social media channels. Public status is a powerful motivator — people will compete for the title.

## Recommended 21-Day Execution Timeline

### Phase 1: Launch (Days 1–3) — Build Excitement

- Announce the challenge across all channels
- Share "Health Ka Tohfa" branding and graphics
- Launch the Healing Link tool
- Explain the Yoga Buddy pairing

### Phase 2: Build Momentum (Days 4–10) — Drive Daily Action

- Daily prompts in the Facebook group
- Feature transformation posters
- Instructor-led invite moments during live sessions
- Start the Buddy Wall

### Phase 3: Peak Push (Days 11–17) — Gamify and Compete

- Launch the "Referral Streak" challenge
- Spotlight top inviters and buddy pairs
- Run the poster contest
- Weekly "Buddy Pair of the Week" feature

### Phase 4: Final Sprint (Days 18–21) — Urgency and Celebration

- Countdown posts: "Sirf 3 din bache hain!"
- Milestone celebrations
- Crown the Health Ambassador
- Closing ceremony recognising all buddy pairs who completed 21 days

## Summary
This campaign aligns self-interest with selflessness. Members aren't asked to "get people to sign up" — they're invited to gift health, share what changed their life, and walk the journey alongside someone they care about. Every tactic — from the Healing Link tool to the Yoga Buddy pairing — makes it easy, emotional, and rewarding to bring someone into the community.

The 21-day structure ensures this isn't a one-day announcement that fades. It's a sustained daily rhythm that builds momentum, creates accountability, and ends with celebration.
