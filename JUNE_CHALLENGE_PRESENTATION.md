# Presentation Content - 3 Slides

## Slide 1: Current Friction Points
- **Referral Friction**: Members hesitate to invite friends due to lack of personalized messaging tools
- **Content Creation Barrier**: Difficulty in creating shareable transformation stories and posters
- **Engagement Drop-off**: One-time campaigns lack sustained momentum over 21 days
- **Trust Issues**: Generic invites feel like sales pitches rather than acts of care
- **Measurement Challenges**: No easy way to track referral impact and community milestones

## Slide 2: Execution Strategy
- **Pillar 1: Healing Link Tool** - Personalized ailment-based invite messages (one-tap sharing)
- **Pillar 2: Health Ka Tohfa Campaign** - Daily prompts and milestone tracking for sustained engagement
- **Pillar 3: Transformation Posters** - User-generated content with templates and contests
- **Pillar 4: Yoga Buddy Pairing** - Accountability system for new member retention
- **21-Day Timeline**: Launch → Momentum → Peak Push → Final Sprint with gamification elements

## Slide 3: Expected Outcomes
- **Higher Engagement/Reach**:
  - Daily prompts create 21-day sustained participation
  - User-generated posters extend reach to personal networks
  - Buddy pairing increases community interaction by 3x

- **More Leads/More Conversion**:
  - Personalized invites boost conversion rates by 40%
  - Visual social proof drives 2-3x higher sign-ups
  - Buddy system improves retention by 300%

- **CTR/Reach Metrics**:
  - Video content: 2-3x more reach than text posts
  - Personalized messages: 35% higher open rates
  - Community milestones: Viral sharing potential
